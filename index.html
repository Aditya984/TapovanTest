Good to know

Dining FAQs

Are meals included in the room rate?

Meals are included only with the AP (meal-inclusive) plan. Room-only rates do not include meals.

What does the AP meal plan include?

An unlimited vegetarian and non-vegetarian buffet. Slot 1 covers lunch, high-tea, dinner and next-day breakfast. Slot 2 covers high-tea, dinner, breakfast and lunch before check-out.

How many people does the dining hall seat?

Our AC dining hall seats 40 to 50 guests. Larger events are served on the lawns.

Can we bring our own caterer for an event?

Our in-house team caters events by default. An outside caterer is allowed with a royalty. Call us for the details.

Planning a meal or an event?

Tell us your group size and date and we will suggest a menu and quote.
