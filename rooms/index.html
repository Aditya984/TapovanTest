Before you book

Room FAQs

Do room rates include meals?

Meals are included only with the AP (meal-inclusive) plan, which covers an unlimited vegetarian and non-vegetarian buffet. Room-only rates do not include meals.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

There are two slots: 12:00 PM to 10:00 AM the next day, or 5:00 PM to 3:00 PM the next day (available on the AP meal-inclusive plan).

How do I get the rate for my dates?

Rates change on weekends and holidays, so call 75594 11323 or message us on WhatsApp with your dates and group size.

Which room is best for a large group?

Dormitories and several room types suit larger groups. Call or WhatsApp with your group size and dates and we will suggest the right mix.
