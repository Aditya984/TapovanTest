Good to know

Wedding and event FAQs

How many guests can the big lawn hold?

The big lawn holds about 1,800 to 2,000 guests in a floating arrangement, which suits weddings and receptions.

Do you have a smaller option for sangeet or birthdays?

Yes. The small lawn suits sangeets, small weddings of 500 to 800 guests, and birthdays.

Do you host destination weddings?

Yes. We host destination weddings, along with haldi, sangeet, receptions, birthdays and corporate events. Guests can stay in our cottages, suites, rooms and dormitories.

Can we use our own caterer?

Our in-house team caters events by default. If you want a different caterer, you can bring one for a royalty. Call us for details.

How much does an event cost?

Every event is planned around the client, so we quote after talking to you. Call 75594 11323 or message us on WhatsApp for a quote.

Planning an event?

Call us with your date and guest count. We will plan it around you and send a quote.
